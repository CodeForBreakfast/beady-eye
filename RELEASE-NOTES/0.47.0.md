## Highlights

**A writable `bdi web` offers only the changes that fit the bead.** Every bead offers a comment and a new priority. A bead that is not closed offers a close and a deferral, and a closed bead offers a reopen. A bead that is not closed and carries the `human` label also offers a response and a dismissal, and its page opens on the response.

**Each change rests as a button until you press it.** The buttons sit in a row at the end of the bead's page, and the priority has its own button where the page shows it. Pressing one opens its form, and only the button inside the form makes the change. A deferral offers Tomorrow, Next monday and In a week beside its date field, and the priority is a choice of P0 to P4.

**The terminal's keys open the same forms on the page.** Press `m`, `x` or another write key while the bead's panel has the focus, and its form opens. The panel takes the focus when the page opens, so a letter typed anywhere else opens nothing.

**Words you are typing survive the page updating.** A form holding words stays open when the bead changes under it, even once that change no longer fits the bead or the page stops showing the bead. It waits, disabled, until the change fits again. Words typed into one form are still lost if you press another button or follow a link.
