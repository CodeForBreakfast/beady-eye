Patch release, **0.35.0 → 0.35.1**.

## Highlights

**A bead told of a review now hears how many inline comments it holds and whether it has a written body.** An approval with inline comments no longer reads as a plain approval. Neither the comments nor the body are quoted.

**A bead hears once that a pull request's checks have passed.** It used to be told twice, once for the delivery and once for the look the delivery prompted.
