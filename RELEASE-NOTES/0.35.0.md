bdi 0.35.0

Minor release, **0.34.0 → 0.35.0**.

## Highlights

**`bdi gates` tells a gate's beads more of what happens on its pull request.** A bead held back by a gate on an open pull request now hears of each new head the author pushes, of every check on the head passing, of the head falling behind a base that requires up-to-date branches, and of each reply or lone inline comment in a review. Each is told once and leaves the gate open. You need do nothing to get these.

**`bdi gates --listen` tells a gate's beads what each webhook delivery says happened.** A delivery used only to prompt a fresh look at GitHub, so a label, a dismissed review or a passing check suite never reached a waiting seat. Now it names who acted and links to what they did, without quoting anyone's words. `docs/configuration.md` has a table of what each delivery tells.

**`bdi gates --listen` settles a pull request on an inline review comment.** Subscribe your webhook to *Pull request review comments* to hear of one at once rather than at the next look. Without the subscription nothing changes.

A token refused a head commit's checks is now reported as unable to see failing checks and unable to see passing checks, on a line each.
