bdi 0.31.1

Patch release, **0.31.0 → 0.31.1**.

## Highlights

**bdi no longer re-reads a tracker every five minutes because it holds a claim with no live lease.** It reads that tracker again when the tracker reports a change. A claim kept alive only by a heartbeat may show as unleased until then. You need do nothing.

Dependency bumps and internal improvements.
