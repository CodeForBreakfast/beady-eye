Minor release, **0.31.1 → 0.32.0**.

## Highlights

**`bdi --ready` lists what you could pick up, and `r` switches to it.** The ready list shows the beads in progress or with a live agent on them, then the beads ready to start, each once and most urgent first. It covers what the forest would draw, or only a bead you name, as in `bdi --ready dun-7`, and the work beneath it. It counts a blocker in another project, and it offers a parent's open children rather than the parent. Its foot counts the beads the forest flags and the projects it could not read, and `/` searches only its own rows.

**Expanding a ready list row shows what its bead unblocks.** It draws each bead waiting on it, up to the root and across projects, and marks which would be unblocked once it closes and which wait on more.

**`bdi --beads --ready` writes the ready list as JSON.** It writes the beads the list draws, in its order, under `in_progress` and `ready`, so `bdi --beads --ready | jq '.ready[].id'` is what you could pick up now.

**A bead window lists a bead waiting on it from another project.** The window on a bead names the beads in other projects `bdi` reads that wait on it under what it blocks, and following one takes you to it.

None of this needs anything from you.
