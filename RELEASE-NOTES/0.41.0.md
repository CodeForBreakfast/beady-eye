bdi 0.41.0

Minor release, **0.40.1 → 0.41.0**.

## Highlights

**`bdi web` shows your trees in a browser.** Run `bdi web --listen 0.0.0.0:8080` and open that address. You get the same trees the terminal draws, and clicking a bead opens it beside them. Each bead also has a page of its own at `/<project>/<bead-id>`, so anything that knows a bead can link to it. The page shows no agents or panes.

**The page keeps itself current.** An open page changes as the trees do, without a reload, and keeps your place, your folds and anything typed in the find field. A browser without scripts gets the same page, drawn once. If a proxy sits in front of `bdi web`, read the README's section on it: the proxy must not buffer the response, and must not drop a connection that has been idle for 15 seconds.

**The page does what `r`, `v`, `/` and `F` do in the terminal.** A menu picks every tree or one of your views, a switch lists only ready beads, a field searches, and a bead's page offers "show only this tree". Each choice is part of the address, so a link keeps it.

**On a phone, the page is laid out for a phone.** A bead's line takes two rows, and depth is drawn as rails down the left. The list menu, Ready and Find sit in a bar at the foot of the screen. An open bead takes the whole screen with its text fitted to the screen's width, and "✕ back" returns you to its row.

**Anyone who can reach the address can read every tracker you configure.** The page asks nobody to sign in, so listen only where everyone who can reach it may read them all. It answers only a request sent to `localhost`, to an address, or to a name you give with `--allow-host`, and refuses every other with `421 Misdirected Request`. Behind a proxy that routes by name, give that name with `--allow-host`.
