bdi 0.13.0

Minor release, **0.12.0 → 0.13.0**.

## Highlights

**The bead window is a drawer against the right edge.** It stands full height from the top row down to the foot, so the forest's spine, glyphs and ids stay readable beside the bead you opened. Nothing to do: `Enter` opens it as before.

**Its head says everything the bead's forest row says, and more.** The badges with their links, each anomaly on a row of its own, the progress fraction, the agent with its state, the labels, the assignee, and when the bead was created, started, updated and closed. Where the head drew the owner's address it now draws the name `bd show` prints.

**A description fills the window rather than keeping bd's wrap.** A hard break still breaks the row, and a table keeps the alignment its author wrote.

**A related bead's id is blue where the forest can take you to it,** which is where `Tab` will stop.

**The window's keys are on the foot row,** as `Esc back ? keys Tab related y id`. The border title says which bead you are on and how far down it you have got.
