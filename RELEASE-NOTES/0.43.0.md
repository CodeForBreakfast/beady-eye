bdi 0.43.0

Minor release, **0.42.0 → 0.43.0**.

## Highlights

**The web view is much faster on a big tracker and on a phone.** Pages and live updates are sent gzipped to any browser that asks for it, which makes them about a tenth of the size. A change to one bead now redraws only that bead's line, so the page keeps your scroll, your focus and what you typed in the search field. Opening the page or coming back to its tab no longer fetches it twice.

**An open bead sits in a panel beside the trees on a wide screen.** At 1100 pixels and wider the bead opens in a panel on the right, with an action bar holding "Show only this tree" and "Copy id". Escape closes it. On a phone the bead takes the whole page, with its action bar at the foot and a link back to the list you came from.

**A bead's page leads with its status and what it waits on.** Its status shows in words at the top, and a blocked bead lists the beads it waits on under its title.

**Project headings stay in view and link onwards.** A project's heading stays at the top of the window while its trees scroll. It says how many beads are left, such as "6 left of 9", and that links to a list of them, most urgent first. Its count of beads to check links to those beads. It shows when the project was read only once that read is two minutes old.

**The beads to check are a list of their own.** The menu offers it with its count, and on a desktop a button beside the Ready switch opens it.

**Tree rows are easier to read and to fold.** Rails show how deep each row hangs, dashed where a bead hangs under one it blocks. The fold is a button of its own, so a click anywhere else on the row opens the bead. Badges, flags and progress bars line up in columns on the right. A flag reads as a sentence, such as "Claimed, untouched for 58 days, no agent on it".

**Lists across several projects say where each bead lives.** Each row names its project, and the project headings fold into one line that names only a project whose read went wrong.

**Showing one tree takes a single line.** The band over it reads "Showing only this tree", with "Show all" at its right. An address naming a bead that no tree holds says "This bead is not in any tree".

**A phone page is titled and tidier.** It opens on the name of the list it shows, its foot bar has larger controls with icons, and a deep tree stops indenting after four levels.

**Status colours appear only on status marks.** In-progress is blue in the web view, so it no longer looks like a flag's amber. The terminal's colours are unchanged.

**A project line rooted at one bead counts that bead's tree.** This holds in the terminal under `F` as well as in the web view, so the line agrees with the bead's own progress.

## Maintenance

Tests of the web page's script in a headless browser, plus dependency bumps.
