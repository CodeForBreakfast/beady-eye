bdi 0.10.0

Minor release, **0.9.0 → 0.10.0**.

## Highlights

**The fold keys come in two cases.** Lower case acts on the selected bead and everything beneath it, upper case on the whole forest: `e`/`E` expand, `c`/`C` collapse, `d`/`D` restore the default fold. `E` and `C` used to act on the subtree, so what they did is now on `e` and `c`, and `d` is new.

A bead that sits under more than one parent is drawn once per parent, and every shut copy now says how many agents, anomalies and unfinished beads it hides.

## Maintenance

CI uploads only what a run built to its cache.
