bdi 0.12.0

Minor release, **0.11.0 → 0.12.0**.

## Highlights

**A bead several others wait on can be opened once instead of under each of them.** The fold default opens every way down to such a bead, so its whole subtree goes on the screen twice over, or more. `s` puts a rule in force under the selected node that opens one copy and rests the rest shut over the same work, and `S` does it across the whole forest. Pressing again cycles the four rules — first reached, shallowest, parent-child and deepest — and the foot names the one in force. A copy resting shut still counts the agents and the anomalies beneath it, so nothing goes missing. Nothing else to do: the forest still starts opening every way down.
