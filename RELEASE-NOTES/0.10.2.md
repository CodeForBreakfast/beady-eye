bdi 0.10.2

Patch release, **0.10.1 → 0.10.2**.

## Highlights

**A big forest is fast now.** Expanding one used to freeze `bdi` for seconds, and every key after that was slow. It now expands and responds instantly however far it is open. Nothing to do; every key does what it did.

**A refresh no longer closes what you opened with `e` or `c`.** Those folds stay as you set them until a bead arrives or leaves beneath them.

Internal improvements.
