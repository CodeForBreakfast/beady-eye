bdi 0.16.0

Minor release, **0.15.0 → 0.16.0**.

## Highlights

**Another project's bead draws beneath the bead waiting on it,** and a bead waiting on another project's unfinished bead is no longer ready. The bead window says `ready` or `blocked by:`.

**A dependency on a bead no tracker holds is drawn where it would hang,** saying why it is missing. beads calls this an orphaned dependency, and so does `bdi` now.

**`bdi --beads` lists each unfinished bead once** with its readiness, blockers, agent and badges, for picking work. `bdi --beads | jq '.beads[] | select(.ready)'` gives the ready list.

**Search moves as you type,** as vim's `incsearch` does. `^G` and `^T` step between matches, the prompt takes a shell's line-editing keys, and `n` and `N` shut what the last step opened.

**A bead that appears in several places in a tree now opens only in its deepest place,** under as much of the work waiting on it as the tree can show. Its other places stay shut. Press `s` to open every place again, as earlier releases did.

**A bead named on the command line starts focused on it,** as `F` does. While the view is focused, the foot row says so and offers `F` to bring back the rest.

## Breaking: `trees[].dangling` under `--json`

If something reads `bdi --json`, `trees[].dangling` is now `trees[].beads_with_orphaned_dependencies`, with the same meaning.

Fixes, tests, docs and internal improvements.
