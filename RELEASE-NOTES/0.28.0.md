bdi 0.28.0

Minor release, **0.27.1 → 0.28.0**. Nothing needs changing to upgrade.

## Highlights

**`bdi bd` defers a bead until a date.** `bdi bd <project> defer <bead> --until <date>` runs `bd defer` against that project's own tracker, as `human respond` already does, so you can snooze a bead in any project bdi reads without knowing where its tracker lives. It takes no flag but `--until`.

**`bdi --beads` says when a deferred bead comes back.** Each bead carries its `defer_until` where it has one, and leaves the key out otherwise, so a caller can tell a bead snoozed to a date from one deferred until someone undefers it.
